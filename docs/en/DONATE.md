# Support iPhoneMirror

[Documentation](README.md) · [简体中文赞赏说明](../zh-CN/DONATE.md)

iPhoneMirror is free and open source. Donations help fund development and maintenance and are optional. You can open the same donation options under **About → Support the author**.

Scan with the corresponding payment app. On a phone, save the original image and use the app's scan-from-album feature. Check that the recipient is the author before confirming. The name shown on the codes is 「修仙之道上的翔（**翔）」.
